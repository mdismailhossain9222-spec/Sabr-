Hero video
----------
Place your full-screen looping hero video here as:
  public/1.mp4   (primary, used by <video src="/1.mp4" />)
  public/2.mp4   (fallback — swap the src if needed)

The video is autoPlay, loop, muted, playsInline. Keep it short (<30s) and
under ~5 MB for fast LCP. If the file is missing the hero just shows the
dark overlay on the pink background.
